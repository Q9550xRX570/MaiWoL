MaiWoL wakes a computer from your phone. The magic packet goes from the phone to your network. There is no account, no ad, and no tracker in between.

What's new in 2.2.5: The card says on, off, or unreachable. The packet count you choose is sent on the local network, and the same count is sent to the WAN or DDNS address when one is saved. The version line reads 16 October Edition.

What it does:
• Wake one computer, or every computer in a group
• Send the chosen packet count on the local network, and again to WAN or DDNS when that address is saved
• Optional SecureOn password
• Show on, off, or unreachable
• Shut down over SSH or an HTTP webhook, only if you turn that on
• Sign in to SSH with a password or a key file, and test it first
• Home screen widgets, a Quick Settings tile, and launcher shortcuts
• One-shot or weekly alarms that come back after a reboot
• Tasker, MacroDroid, and adb intents
• Groups you can reorder
• Optional PIN or fingerprint lock for the app and the widgets
• Show, mask, or hide MAC, IP, and port on the cards
• Your own backup: plain JSON, or an encrypted .maiwol file
• DNS lookup, ping, and an optional Shizuku scan for MAC addresses without root

MaiWoL does not phone home.
