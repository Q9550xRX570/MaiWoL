MaiWoL is a modern, lightweight, and privacy-focused Wake-on-LAN Android application built with Jetpack Compose.

Features include:
• LAN Broadcast and WAN/DDNS Magic Packet dispatch
• SecureOn 6-byte password support
• Internal Date/Time & Weekly Recurring Scheduling
• External automation support (Tasker / MacroDroid / ADB)
• Real-time status polling via ICMP & OS service ports
• Card privacy customization (Mask/Hide sensitive network details)
• SHA-256 PIN & Biometric App Lock with FLAG_SECURE window protection
• Shizuku / ADB integration for MAC discovery
• 100% FOSS with zero trackers and zero telemetry.
