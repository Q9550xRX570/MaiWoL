MaiWoL is a modern, enterprise-grade, lightweight, and 100% Free and Open Source (FOSS) Wake-on-LAN suite for Android.

Major Features in v2.0.0:
• LAN Broadcast and WAN/DDNS Magic Packet dispatch with burst pacing
• Enterprise SecureOn 6-byte password injection
• 2x1 Card & 1x1 Quick Wake Home Screen Widgets (with lock protection)
• Quick Settings Notification Shade Tile for instant device wake-ups
• Dynamic App Launcher Shortcuts
• AES-256 Encrypted & Plain Backup / Restore (.maiwol)
• Precision Date/Time & Weekly Recurring Scheduling
• External automation support (Tasker / MacroDroid / ADB intents)
• Multi-tier real-time status polling via ICMP & OS core ports (SMB, RPC, RDP, SSH)
• Card privacy customization (Show / Mask / Hide)
• Cryptographic SHA-256 PIN & Biometric App Lock with FLAG_SECURE protection
• Shizuku / ADB integration for rootless MAC discovery
• Built-in DNS Resolver and Ping Latency Diagnostic Tools
• 100% FOSS with zero trackers and zero telemetry.
