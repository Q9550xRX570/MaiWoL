Status labels now need real proof: Online only when SSH, a Windows service or ping actually answers, with the reason shown on the card. Add/Edit has a Connection Check that works without remote shutdown. SSH can sign in with a key file of any extension (OpenSSH, PEM, PuTTY .ppk, Ed25519) and be tested without shutting down. Wake packets also go to the local broadcast. Android backup no longer includes devices or the app PIN.
